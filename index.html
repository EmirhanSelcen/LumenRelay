Lumen Relay

A procedural HTML5 Canvas game for the Computer Graphics final project.

Goal
Catch matching signal prisms and avoid static pulses.
Controls
A / D or Arrow Keys, Space to start, P to pause, M to mute.
Graphics
Transformations, animation, particles, parallax, collision, procedural rendering.

Match the active frequency shown at the top of the canvas. A full relay meter triggers a short beam sweep that clears dangerous pulses.
